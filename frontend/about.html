About Us

Meet the team behind BlockLearn — a drag-and-drop programming learning platform for PNHS ICT students

Our Mission

To provide an accessible, engaging, and effective programming learning platform that empowers PNHS ICT students to master coding fundamentals through visual, drag-and-drop interaction.

Our Vision

To provide Grade 11 ICT students with a beginner-friendly learning platform that introduces the fundamentals of programming through interactive lessons and activities. BlockLearn aims to help students with little or no programming background develop basic coding skills, build confidence, and reduce culture shock when studying programming.
